# Oracles of the family "order": the order at a tick's input fixed point.
# Written by hand from the design text, before any run.
# Record lines stand in the order applied: the commands first, in the order
# the scenario gives them, then the arrivals by asset identifier (A, B, G).
# A fall is derived from the table and is no element of a record.
# An entry line with no want line under it is an entry that holds no want.
# A tick block with no entry line states an empty table.

scenario order/want-and-arrival
# The want is applied before the arrival of the same record, so the arrival
# answers it: A is available from tick 4, under its want.
tick 4
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1

scenario order/release-and-arrival
tick 2
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
# The release is applied first: the arrival at A.2 finds no want of A, changes
# nothing and emits no event. A keeps its hash through tick 5, with no want.
tick 5
record release A source:host
record arrival A A.2
entry A available A.1
# A falls at the input fixed point of tick 6 and leaves: the table is empty.
tick 6

scenario order/want-again-after-release
tick 2
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
# The last want of A ends during tick 5: A keeps its entry, with no want.
tick 5
record release A source:host
entry A available A.1
# The record of tick 6 is applied before the falls: A has a want again, does
# not fall, and stays at its hash without a gap. No event.
tick 6
record want A source:host required
entry A available A.1
want source:host required

scenario order/want-again-after-despawn
tick 2
record want A entity:E1 required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want entity:E1 required
event arrived A A.1
# The want of E1 ends with the despawn, at tick 5: A keeps its entry, with
# no want.
tick 5
record despawn E1
entry A available A.1
# Wanted again at the tick of its fall, by another maker and as optional:
# A stays at its hash without a gap. No event.
tick 6
record want A source:host optional
entry A available A.1
want source:host optional

scenario order/want-again-live
# A live loading capability: the answer to the want of tick 2 is in the
# record of tick 3, and tick 2 runs with A unanswered.
tick 2
record want A source:host required
entry A unavailable
want source:host required
tick 3
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
tick 5
record release A source:host
entry A available A.1
tick 6
record want A source:host required
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
# Reading taken: the loading capability answers the want of tick 6 as it
# answers any want, with no exemption for an asset already in force. The
# record of tick 7 holds one arrival of A at the hash in force, which changes
# nothing and emits no event.
tick 7
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
# The records of ticks 8, 9 and 10 are empty.

scenario order/accumulated-want-and-arrival
tick 3
record want G source:host required
entry G unavailable
want source:host required
# Gated on G from the boundary after tick 3. The pending want of A is left
# out by the gate, and the pending arrival of A answers nothing required.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The arrival of G joins and opens the gate. The whole record is applied in
# order: the want, then the arrivals of A and of G.
tick 4
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
record arrival G G.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
entry G available G.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
event arrived G G.1

scenario order/accumulated-release-and-arrival
tick 2
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
tick 3
record want G source:host required
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
entry G unavailable
want source:host required
# Gated on G: A is required and answered, G is required and not answered.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The release is applied first: the arrival at A.2 finds no want of A, changes
# nothing and emits no event. The arrival of G answers its want.
tick 4
record release A source:host
record arrival A A.2
record arrival G G.1
entry A available A.1
entry G available G.1
want source:host required
event arrived G G.1
# A falls at the input fixed point of tick 5 and leaves.
tick 5
entry G available G.1
want source:host required

scenario order/accumulated-want-again
tick 1
record want A source:host required
record arrival A A.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
event arrived A A.1
# The last want of A ends during tick 3: A keeps its entry, with no want.
tick 3
record want G source:host required
record release A source:host
entry A available A.1
entry G unavailable
want source:host required
# Gated on G. No tick runs while the gate is closed, so no fall is applied:
# A stays in the table with no want.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# Tick 4 applies its record before the falls: A has a want again and stays at
# its hash without a gap, with no event of its own.
tick 4
record want A source:host required
record arrival G G.1
entry A available A.1
want source:host required
entry G available G.1
want source:host required
event arrived G G.1

scenario order/gate-release-and-arrival
tick 3
record want G source:host required
entry G unavailable
want source:host required
# The step without waiting comes before anything is pending.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The pending release of the required want counts as the answer of G, so the
# gate opens. The release is applied first: the arrival finds no want of G,
# changes nothing and emits no event. G never becomes available.
# Reading taken: an entry whose last want ended keeps its place for one tick
# whatever its availability, so G stands here as unavailable with no want.
tick 4
record release G source:host
record arrival G G.1
entry G unavailable
# G leaves the table at the input fixed point of tick 5: the table is empty.
tick 5

scenario order/gate-arrival-and-new-want
tick 3
record want G source:host required
entry G unavailable
want source:host required
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The pending arrival answers G and the pending want of B is left out by the
# gate, so the gate opens. The want is applied before the arrival; tick 4
# runs with B unanswered.
tick 4
record want B source:host required
record arrival G G.1
entry B unavailable
want source:host required
entry G available G.1
want source:host required
event arrived G G.1
# The want of B gates from the boundary after its tick.
blocked 5 awaiting B
tick 5
record arrival B B.1
entry B available B.1
want source:host required
entry G available G.1
want source:host required
event arrived B B.1

scenario order/gate-pending-despawn
tick 3
record want G entity:E1 required
entry G unavailable
want entity:E1 required
# The first step without waiting, before anything is pending.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The second step without waiting: the pending despawn of the owner is not
# seen by the gate, which stays closed on G.
blocked 4 awaiting G
# The arrival of G joins and opens the gate. The despawn is applied first and
# ends the want of E1: the arrival finds no want of G, changes nothing and
# emits no event. G never becomes available.
# Reading taken, as above: G keeps its entry for one tick, unavailable and
# with no want.
tick 4
record despawn E1
record arrival G G.1
entry G unavailable
# G leaves the table at the input fixed point of tick 5: the table is empty.
tick 5
