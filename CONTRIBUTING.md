# Contributing to Ludenea

Thank you for your interest in Ludenea.

## Pull requests

External pull requests are not merged until a contributor license agreement is
in place. The agreement is needed because parts of the repository (the
`editor/` directory) are licensed under the AGPLv3 and may also be offered
under a commercial license. Until it exists, a pull request may be read and
discussed but will be closed without merging. This file will be updated when
the agreement is ready.

## Issues

Bug reports, questions about the design and feature ideas are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/ludenea/ludenea/issues). Please search existing
issues first and use the matching template.

Security vulnerabilities must not be reported in public issues; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part in the project is expected to follow the
[code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Licensing of contributions

Unless stated otherwise, a contribution to a path outside `editor/` is
licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0, and a contribution to `editor/` is licensed
under the AGPLv3, matching the license of the files it changes.
